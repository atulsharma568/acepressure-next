AcePressure starter sounds
==========================

These 8 sound effects were made for AcePressure entirely from code: no
samples, recordings or third-party audio were used.

They are released into the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so you can use them
on streams and in videos, including monetised ones.

  vine-boom.mp3        Vine Boom
  air-horn.mp3         Air Horn
  cash-register.mp3    Cash Register
  victory-fanfare.mp3  Victory Fanfare
  clap.mp3             Clap
  game-over.mp3        Game Over
  ba-dum-tss.mp3       Ba Dum Tss
  sad-trombone.mp3     Sad Trombone

Admin -> Sound library -> "Add starter sounds" copies them into your
library. Each copy gets a new name in your data/media folder, so the files
here are never changed or deleted. Viewers can pick them right away; rename,
reprice, replace or delete them like any of your own sounds. Deleted one by
mistake? Click "Add starter sounds" again to get it back.

The generators that made them are in scripts/starter_sounds/.
